Make it yours

Each effect is a short JSON recipe. Pixelbolt bakes the same simulation into sprite sheets, atlases, GIFs and Godot 4 files for your game, and your AI agent can write and tune the recipe for you: pixelbolt compile campfire.fluid.json --out campfire.json on the command line, or pixel_compile over MCP.

Everything runs in your browser and nothing you do is sent anywhere: a link keeps what you did after its #, which browsers never send to a server. The engine and effects load from jsDelivr.
