Pixelbolt

66 pixel art effects, sprites and scenes, every one a recipe

These are JSON recipes rendered by Pixelbolt 0.11.0. Open any in the playground in your browser with nothing installed, edit it and download a sprite sheet, atlas or GIF. Pixelbolt is free and MIT-licensed. Your agent can make new ones through the MCP server or the skill: source, setup and documentation.

  • Phase changes
  • Electricity
  • Fire, smoke and gas
  • Liquids
  • Particle and spell effects
  • Waves
  • Characters and motion
  • Hand-drawn sprites
  • Tile sets
  • Scenes

One-shot effects play once in a game; here their previews loop, with a 0.8 s pause before each replay. Each card shows up to four animations; the recipe includes those listed under “also”. Export commands write sprite sheets, atlases, Godot files and frames; tile sets include terrain exports, and scenes include asset bundles and numbered scene frames.
