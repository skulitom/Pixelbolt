**The recipe format** (what `pixel_help` teaches and `pixel_validate` enforces): required `version: 1`, `name`, `width`, `height` (1 to 256) and `frames` (1 to 256). `name` and every frame, symbol and animation name start with a letter, then letters, digits, hyphens or underscores. `palette` maps keys to `#RGB`, `#RGBA`, `#RRGGBB`, `#RRGGBBAA` or `transparent`; keys may be longer than one character, but only single-character keys can be used in `grid` rows, and `.` and space mean transparent there. A frame is `{name, duration?, from?, ops?, layers?, pixels?}`: `duration` is 1 to 60000 ms (default 100), and `from` copies an earlier frame by name. Layers are `{name?, opacity?, x?, y?, ops?}`. `symbols` maps names to equal-width text rows of palette keys, which `stamp` draws. The ops (all of them are in the facts below) include `pixel` (x, y, color), `rect` and `ellipse` (x, y, w, h, color, filled?), `line` (x, y, x2, y2, color), `grid` (x, y, rows), `stamp` (x, y, symbol) and `box` (x, y, w, d, h, top, left, right). `animations` maps names to `{frames: [frame names], direction?, loop?}`. Unknown fields are errors. An fx source is `{format: "pixelforge-fx", version: 1, name, width, height, palette?, symbols?, effects: {<effect name>: {...}}}`, where the optional `symbols` names the particle shapes its emitters draw. A fluid source is the same with `format: "pixelforge-fluid"` (smoke, fire, explosions, magic and water), except that its `width` and `height` are 4 to 128 and `symbols` names obstacle shapes. In both, `effects` is keyed by effect name. A wave source (`format: "pixelforge-wave"`: looping interference and portal effects) has `version: 1`, `name`, `width` and `height` (1 to 128), `palette` and 1 to 16 named `effects`, and no `symbols`; `pixel_help`'s `wave` topic is its schema. Crystal (`pixelforge-crystal`), falling-sand (`pixelforge-powder`) and rigid-body (`pixelforge-bodies`) sources compile the same way, each outlined by the `pixel_help` topic of its name.
